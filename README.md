# Grade 4 PEP 🇯🇲

Interactive Grade 4 PEP preparation platform aligned to Jamaica's curriculum.

## What this version includes
- Supabase-backed parent sign-up and sign-in
- Revised Grade 4 pricing plans
- Manual payment submission with admin verification
- Automatic subscription activation and expiry dates after approval
- Support for up to 4 students on the Premium Family plan

## Grade 4 plans
- Free
- Standard Weekly — JMD 1,000
- Standard Monthly — JMD 3,000
- Premium Family Monthly — JMD 10,000 for up to 4 students

## Important
This Grade 4 programme is sold separately and does not include Grade 5.
